East Africa Trail Seekers

Project Site Plan & Design Architecture

1. Site Overview

Site Name

East Africa Trail Seekers (Domain Concept: eatrailseekers.org)

Reason for Site Name

This name was selected because it immediately communicates the regional focus (East Africa) and speaks directly to our target audience—adventurers and outdoor enthusiasts looking for reliable hiking trails, community expeditions, and local guide resources.

Site Purpose

The primary purpose of East Africa Trail Seekers is to empower outdoor enthusiasts by providing accurate trail data, live weather alerts, guided expedition schedules, and a community membership network for local and international hikers exploring Uganda and East Africa.

2. Target Audience & Scenarios

Target Audience

Hikers, eco-tourists, weekend adventurers, and professional trek guides aged 18–55. They predominantly access the internet via mobile devices while traveling or planning weekend trips.

User Scenarios / Questions

  • Scenario 1: "What are the difficulty ratings and elevation gains for trails near Mount Elgon?"
  • Scenario 2: "How can I register for the upcoming group expedition to the Rwenzori Mountains?"
  • Scenario 3: "What gear recommendations and weather forecasts should I prepare for before starting a hike?"
